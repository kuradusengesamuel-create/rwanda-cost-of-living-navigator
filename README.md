# Rwanda Cost-of-Living Navigator

> *"How much is inflation really costing me this month?"*
> This platform answers that question — personally, clearly, and in real time.

---

## The Problem

Every month, Rwanda's National Institute of Statistics (NISR) publishes 
inflation figures. But what does "14.5% inflation" actually mean for a 
family in Musanze, a shopkeeper in Nyamirambo, or a government planner 
in Kigali?

The honest answer is: nobody knows — because the national average treats 
everyone the same. A family spending most of its income on food feels 
inflation very differently from one spending mostly on rent or transport.

This platform fixes that.

---

## What It Does

The Rwanda Cost-of-Living Navigator takes official NISR data and turns it 
into something personal and useful. You enter what you actually spend money 
on, and the platform tells you:

- What YOUR inflation rate is — not the national average
- Which spending category is hurting your household the most right now
- What went up, stayed the same, or came down this month
- What to expect in the next 1 to 3 months
- How your situation compares to other households like yours

No economics degree needed. No technical jargon. Just clear, honest 
information you can act on.

## Three Tiers, One Platform

### Tier 1 — Personal Tool
Enter your monthly income and what you spend on food, transport, rent, 
health, and education. Get your personal inflation rate, a spotlight on 
your biggest pressure, and practical budgeting tips. Share your results 
directly on WhatsApp.

### Tier 2 — Business Tool
See which cost categories are rising fastest, get a signal for wage review 
conversations, and plan ahead with a short-term cost forecast.

### Tier 3 — Government Dashboard
A color-coded district pressure index built by combining live CPI trends 
with EICV7 household spending patterns — showing where cost-of-living 
pressure is highest and which income groups are most at risk.

---

## Data Sources

| Dataset | Type | Purpose |
|---------|------|---------|
| NISR Consumer Price Index (CPI) | Monthly, live | Powers all inflation calculations |
| NISR EICV7 Household Survey (2023–2024) | One-time load | District spending basket weights |

The platform auto-refreshes every month when NISR releases new CPI data — 
so it stays useful long after the hackathon ends.

---

## Forecast — Clearly Labelled

Short-term inflation projections (1 to 3 months ahead) are included for 
each spending category. Every forecast is clearly marked as a model 
estimate — not a confirmed local price. We believe in being honest about 
what data can and cannot tell us.
